## 2. Principal Role

The Principal has full administrative control over the institution's data, including staff, subjects, and global settings.

### 2.1 Principal Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of institutional attendance performance, including total students, low-attendance alerts, and daily summaries.

![Principal Dashboard](file:///home/darkemperor/.gemini/antigravity/brain/7e6fb73a-581a-4cdd-86d7-62852dbc0342/principal_dashboard_1775445636085.png)

### 2.2 Staff Management
Administrators can add new staff members, manage existing profiles, and deactivate accounts. All data-modifying actions include a **Preview** or **Confirmation** step.

![Staff Management](file:///home/darkemperor/.gemini/antigravity/brain/7e6fb73a-581a-4cdd-86d7-62852dbc0342/principal_add_staff_1775445656966.png)

### 2.3 Subject Management
Manage academic subjects and their assignments. New subjects can be added with a preview modal to ensure data accuracy.

![Subject Management](file:///home/darkemperor/.gemini/antigravity/brain/7e6fb73a-581a-4cdd-86d7-62852dbc0342/principal_add_subject_1775445679512.png)

### 2.4 Holiday Marking
A centralized calendar to mark institutional holidays and special working Saturdays.

## 4. Safety Features: Preview & Confirmation

To prevent accidental data entry or loss, the system implements a mandatory "Review & Save" workflow for all critical operations.

### 4.1 Preview Modals
Before any data is committed to the database, a preview modal displays the information to be saved. This ensures that the user can verify details before final submission.

![Staff Preview Modal](file:///home/darkemperor/.gemini/antigravity/brain/7e6fb73a-581a-4cdd-86d7-62852dbc0342/confirm_staff_preview_modal_1775406789475.png)

### 4.2 Confirmation Dialogs
For destructive actions (like deactivating a staff member or deleting a subject), a clear, styled confirmation dialog is presented.

![Confirmation Dialog](file:///home/darkemperor/.gemini/antigravity/brain/7e6fb73a-581a-4cdd-86d7-62852dbc0342/confirm_staff_details_modal_1775443167653.png)
